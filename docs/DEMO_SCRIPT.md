# HomeOps AI v0.4 — 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — 3-second product story

Show the clean Home view.

Say:

> “HomeOps starts with one question: what needs fixing? The customer does not need to understand procurement, workflows or AI tooling.”

Enter:

`My AC isn't cooling. Handle it.`

## 0:20–0:55 — Recommendation first

Show the recommended provider card:

- KlimaPro
- $95
- rating
- arrival window
- plain-English reason

Say:

> “HomeOps compares suitable options but presents one recommendation first. The customer can still compare all providers when they want more detail.”

Click **Approve KlimaPro — $95**.

## 0:55–1:20 — Booked with a visible agreed price

Show:

- provider booked
- appointment window
- agreed price $95

Say:

> “The agreed price remains visible throughout the repair so the customer knows what they approved.”

Click **The work is finished**.

## 1:20–1:55 — Cost-control hero moment

Click **Review final bill — $135**.

Show:

> **The final bill is $40 higher than agreed**

with:

- agreed price $95
- final bill $135
- extra cost $40

Say:

> “HomeOps does not silently accept an unexpected increase. It tells the customer exactly what changed and recommends the next action.”

Click **Ask provider to explain**.

## 1:55–2:25 — Human control

Show that payment remains on hold.

Say:

> “The customer stays in control. An extra charge requires explanation or explicit human approval.”

## 2:25–2:50 — Judge evidence without UX clutter

Click **View demo details**.

Show:

- MCP status
- 12 tools
- audit trail
- deterministic policy decision
- architecture

Say:

> “The technical evidence is available to judges, but it does not burden the normal customer experience.”

## 2:50–3:00 — Safety stop

Run the safety scenario:

`There is smoke coming from my air conditioner.`

Show normal sourcing stopping.

Close:

> “HomeOps combines conversational service orchestration with explicit safety and cost governance.”
